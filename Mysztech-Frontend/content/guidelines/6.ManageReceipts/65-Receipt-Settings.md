Receipt settings can be configured from the Management Hub.

**Don't know how to access Management Hub?** *[Click here to learn how to access Management Hub.](http://localhost:5173/docs?id=How-to-access-Management-Hub)*

1\.	Go to **Settings** in the Management Hub and find and select **Receipt Settings.**

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.5/Picture1-6.5.png>)

2\.	Choose your **Outlet**.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.5/Picture2-6.5.png>)

3\.	Press the **Upload** button under the receipt logo section and select the logo file from your device to add your shop logo to printed receipts.

**Note**: Allowed file: jpg, jpeg, png (max 10MB). Will be compressed and converted to grayscale for optimal printing.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.5/Picture3-6.5.png>)

4\.	Press the **Upload** button under the **Email Logo** section and select the logo file you want to use to add a logo for emailed receipts.

**Note**: Allowed file: jpg, jpeg, png.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.5/Picture4-6.5.png>)

5\.	To customize the receipt header and receipt footer, enter or edit the required information in the **Receipt Header** section and **Receipt Footer** section.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.5/Picture5-6.5.png>)

6\. Press **Save** to save and apply the changes.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.5/Picture6-6.5.png>)
