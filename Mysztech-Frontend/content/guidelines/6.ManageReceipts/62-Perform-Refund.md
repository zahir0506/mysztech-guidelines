The **Refund** function allows you to refund selected items from a completed receipt.

**To Process a Refund**

1\.	Press the **Menu** button.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.2/Picture1-6.2.png>)

2\.	Select **Refund**.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.2/Picture2-6.2.png>)

3\.	Select the **item** you want to refund (e.g. **NG Paprik Ayam**).

Note: If only 1 out of 2 **NG Paprik Ayam** orders need to be refunded, tap **NG Paprik Ayam** just once to select the item.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.2/Picture3-6.2.png>)

4\.	Select the appropriate **Payment Type** (e.g. Cash).

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.2/Picture4-6.2.png>)

5\.	Press **Refund button** to complete the refund. The Refund Amount is displayed.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.2/Picture5-6.2.png>)

6\.	After the refund is completed, a **Refunded Receipt** will be generated with a refund label. Optionally,  press **Print Receipt** to print the refunded receipt **OR** press **Send Receipt to Email** to send the refunded receipt to your email address.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.2/Picture6-6.2.png>)
