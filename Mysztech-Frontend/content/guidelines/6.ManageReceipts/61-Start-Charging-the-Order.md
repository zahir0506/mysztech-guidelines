You can view and manage all completed receipts from the **Receipt** section.

**To View a Receipt**

1\.	Press the Menu button.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.1/Picture1-6.1.png>)

2\.	Select the Receipt tab.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.1/Picture2-6.1.png>)

3\.	A list of all completed receipts will be displayed.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.1/Picture3-6.1.png>)

4\.	Select any receipt to view the items included in the order.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.1/Picture4-6.1.png>)

**Filter Receipts**

Press the Filter button to filter receipts based on the following options:

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.1/Picture5-6.1.png>)

**Date Range**

1\.	Press **Select Date Range** and select the Start Date and End Date.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.1/Picture6-6.1.png>)

2\.	Adjust the **Start Time** and **End Time** for more precise filtering. Then, press Ok to confirm.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.1/Picture7-6.1.png>)

**Payment Type**

1\.	Press **Select Payment Type.**

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.1/Picture8-6.1.png>)

2\.	Select the **Payment Type** you want to filter (e.g. Cash)  and close the tab.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.1/Picture9-6.1.png>)

**Order Option — such as Dine In or Take Away**

1\.	Press **Select Order Option.**

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.1/Picture10-6.1.png>)

2\.	Select the **Order Option** you want to filter (e.g. **Dine In** or **Take Away**) and close the tab.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.1/Picture11-6.1.png>)

3\.	After confirming your selections, press **Apply Filter** to apply the changes and view the filtered receipts.

**Search by Receipt ID**

**Note:** You can also search for a specific receipt using its Receipt ID. A unique **Receipt ID** is automatically generated and assigned to receipts.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.1/Picture12-6.1.png>)

**Receipt Options**

After selecting a receipt, you can optionally:

**Send Receipt to Email** — send the receipt to an email address.

1\.	Tap Send Receipt to Email and enter your email address.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.1/Picture13-6.1.png>)

2\.	The receipt will be sent to the email address provided.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.1/Picture15-6.1.png>)

**Print Receipt** —  Optionally, you can print the receipt again by pressing the **Print Receipt** button.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.1/Picture16-6.1.png>)
