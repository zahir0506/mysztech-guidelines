The Change Payment Type function allows you to correct the payment method recorded on a completed receipt.

**When to Use Change Payment Type**

A customer initially says they will pay by **Cash**, so the receipt is created with Cash as the payment type. However, the customer later checks their wallet and finds that they do not have enough cash. They decide to pay using **QR** instead.

In this situation, you can change the payment type on the receipt from **Cash** to **QR.**

**Important:** The payment type should be updated to match the actual payment method used. This helps maintain accurate and efficient payment tracking.

**To Change the Payment Type**

1\.	Press the **Menu** button.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.3/Picture1-6.3.png>)

2\.	Select **Change Payment Type.**

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.3/Picture2-6.3.png>)

3\.	Select the correct **Payment Type**. For example, if the original payment type was Cash and the customer paid by **QR**, select **QR** and press **Save**.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.3/Picture3-6.3.png>)

4\.	You have successfully changed the payment type of the receipt. You can verify the change by checking the updated **Payment Type** on the receipt.

![](</admin/6. Manage Receipts/6.3/Picture4-6.3.png>)
