1\.	Back in the MYSZTECH POS system, select the page where you want to add the item, then do a long press on the layout to begin placing it.

![](</admin/4. Sales Screen and Items/4.4/Picture1-4.4.png>)

2\.	Press the ‘+’ button at the spot in the layout where you want the item to be added.

![](</admin/4. Sales Screen and Items/4.4/Picture2-4.4.png>)

3\.	Search for the item or category by its name and select it to add it into the layout.

![](</admin/4. Sales Screen and Items/4.4/Picture3-4.4.png>)

4\.	Click the button at the bottom to save the changes and confirm the item has been added to the POS layout.

![](</admin/4. Sales Screen and Items/4.4/Picture4-4.4.png>)

5\.	You have successfully added the item or category to the POS layout.

![](</admin/4. Sales Screen and Items/4.4/Picture5-4.4.png>)
