**Pre-Upgrade Prerequisites**

* **Data Sync:** Ensure your device is connected to the internet and all offline sales transactions have been fully synced to the cloud server.
* **OS Requirement:** Android 10 or higher is recommended for optimal stability and performance.
* **Free Storage:** Ensure at least 100 MB of free storage space is available on your device.

**Step-by-Step Upgrade Process:**

Via Google Play Store:

* Open the Google Play Store app on your POS terminal.
* Search for **Mysztech POS**.
* Tap the Download button.
* Wait for the download and installation to complete.
* Launch the app.

**Post-Upgrade Checklist:**

* **Re-login Check:** Log back into your account if prompted by the system.
* **Hardware Test:** Perform a quick test print and verify cash drawer connection.
* **Catalog Check:** Verify that item lists, prices, and stock counts display accurately.
