Don’t know how to access Management Hub, *click here to learn how to access Management Hub*

1\.	Go to Settings > Features. Here, you can turn different features on or off based on your business needs.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-145014.png>)

2\.	**Time Clock**

Turn on Time Clock to track staff clock-in and clock-out times and calculate their total working hours.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-145054.png>)

3\.	**Open Orders**

Turn on Open Orders to allow staff to save and edit orders before completing the payment.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-145129.png>)

4\.	**Department Printers**

Use Department Printers to send orders to different printers or displays based on the department, such as Kitchen, Beverages, and others.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-145210.png>)

5\.	**Order Options**

Turn on Order Options to allow orders to be marked as Dine In, Online, or Delivery.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-145244.png>)

6\.	**Layout Management**

Turn Layout Management on or off to control whether the layout management feature is available on the POS device.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-145317.png>)
