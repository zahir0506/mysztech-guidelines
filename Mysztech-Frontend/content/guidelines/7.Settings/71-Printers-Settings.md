Before connecting a printer, you need to create a **Department Printer** in Management Hub. The Department Printer is used to select where the order should be printed.

1\.	Log in to **Management Hub**.

Note: If you do not know how to access Management Hub, *click here to learn how to access Management Hub.*

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-112439.png>)

2\.	Go to **Settings > Department Printers.**

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-112729.png>)

3\.	Click **New Department Printer** to create a new Department Printer.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-112827.png>)

4\.	Enter the **Department Printer Name** and select the **categories** that you want to print.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-112956.png>)

5\.	Check the information and click **Create** to save the Department Printer.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-113432.png>)

6\.	Go back to the **POS System** and go to **Settings > Printers.**

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-113550.png>)

7\.	Click the **+ (Plus)** button to add a new printer.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-114449.png>)

8\.	Select the **Connection Type** of your printer: **Bluetooth, Ethernet, or USB**. For a Bluetooth printer, enter the **Printer Name**, click **Scan for Printer**, and select your printer.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-114931.png>)

9\.	For an **Ethernet** printer, enter the **Printer Name, Printer Model, and Printer IP Address.**

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-115049.png>)

10\.	For a **USB** printer, enter the **Printer Name and Printer Model**, click Scan for Printer, and select your printer.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-120715.png>)

11\.	Select the **Paper Width**. You can choose **58mm** or **80mm**.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-121350.png>)

12\.	Choose whether to **enable or disable Print Receipt and Bills.**

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-121705.png>)

13\.	Choose whether to **enable or disable Automatically Print Receipt.**

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-121705.png>)

14\.	Choose whether to **enable or disable Print Orders.**

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-121908.png>)

15\.	If **Print Orders** is enabled, select the **Department Printer** that you created earlier.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-121958.png>)

16\.	Click **Print Test** to check if the printer is working correctly.If the test print is successful, click **Save** to connect the printer.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-123001.png>)

17\.	To check the print receipt queue, go to **Settings > Print Receipt Queue.**

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-123109.png>)

18\.	The print receipt queue is divided into **All, Success, Pending, Processing, and Failed**. Click a section to view the print queues under that section.

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-123549.png>)

19\.	To delete a print queue, **select the print queue first**, then click **Bulk Delete.**

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-123757.png>)

20\.	To print a receipt again, **select the print queue first**, then click **Bulk Reprint.**

![](</admin/7. Settings/Screenshot-2026-09-22-123929.png>)
