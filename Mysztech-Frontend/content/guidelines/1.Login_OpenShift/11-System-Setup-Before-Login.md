Before accessing the POS system, complete the required setup in the Management Hub/Backoffice. This includes:

\>Create the required **Roles** for staff.

Before using the POS system, the required staff, roles, POS devices and other settings must be configured in the **Management Hub.**

Don’t  know how to access the Management Hub, *Click Here to learn how to access the Management Hub.*

**Create Roles**

1\.	In the Management Hub, scroll down to **Employee** and select **Roles**. The Roles page will display all roles that have already been created.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-093339.png>)

2\.	Click **New Role** to create a new role.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-093435.png>)

3\.	Enter the **Role Name** ( e.g. Staff).

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-093530.png>)

4\.	Set the permissions for the role under the **Management Hub** and **POS App** sections.

Each permission controls what the assigned staff can access or perform.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-093625.png>)

**POS App**

To enable a permission, **toggle the button on**. To prevent the staff from using a permission, **toggle the button off**.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-094712.png>)

**Management Hub**

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-095141.png>)

6\.	Review all permissions and make sure they match the staff member's responsibilities.

**Note:** For example, if staff should not be allowed to open the cash drawer without making a sale, keep **Open Cash Drawer Without Making Sale turned off.**

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-095340.png>)

7\.	Click **Create** to create the role. A confirmation message will appear when the role has been successfully created

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-095448.png>)

**Manage Existing Roles**

After creating a role, the role will appear on the Roles dashboard.

•	Use the **Search** bar to find a specific role by name.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-095716.png>)

•	Click **Edit** on a role to modify its permissions.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-095825.png>)

•	Update the required permissions and **Save** the changes.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-095920.png>)

\>	Create **Staff IDs** and assign the appropriate roles. 

Creating Staff Account

1\.	Go to **Employee > Staff**. Here, you can view all staff accounts that have already been created.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-100803.png>)

2\.	Click ‘New Staff’ 

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-100853.png>)

3\.	Select the staff member's **Role** (e.g. **Staff**). Then, enter the staff member's **Name**.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-101017.png>)

4\.	If the selected role has **POS Access** enabled, a **POS PIN** will be required.  

**Note:** Each staff account must have a different POS PIN.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-101125.png>)

5\.	 Enter a **6-digit PIN** that the staff member can remember.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-101410.png>)

6\.	Optionally, enter the staff member's **Email** and **Phone No**.

**Note: If Management Hub Access** is enabled for the selected role, the staff member's **Email** and **Password** will be required.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-102248.png>)

7\.	Select the **Outlet** where the staff member is assigned.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-102355.png>)

8\.	Check the details and click **Create** to create the staff account. After the account is created, you will be redirected to the **Staff Dashboard**.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-102538.png>)

**Manage Staff**

In the **Staff Dashboard**, you can:

•	Search for a staff member by their **Name**.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-104533.png>)

•	Use the Filter button to filter staff by their **Role**.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-104642.png>)

•	Remove a staff member's active shift if necessary. If you need to forcefully close a staff member's active shift.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-104725.png>)

\>	Register the **POS Device** that will be used. 

A **POS Device** must be selected before starting a shift. Each device must have a registered POS Device to use the Mysztech POS.

1\.	Go to Settings > POS Devices. Here, you can view all registered POS Device.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-104831.png>)

2\.	Click New Pos Device.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-104933.png>)

3\.	Enter a **Code Number** (e.g. **001, 002**). Then, enter a **Name** for the POS Device (e.g. **Main POS**).

**Note:** The **Code Number** must be unique for each POS Device. This unique code will be used in the receipt number.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-105205.png>)

4\.	Select the **Outlet** where the POS Device will be used.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-105246.png>)

5\.	Click **Create** and you have successfully created a POS Device.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-105340.png>)

**Manage POS Devices**

You can manage your registered POS Devices from the **POS Devices** page.

•	Use the **Search Bar** to search for a specific POS Device.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-105446.png>)

•	Click **Filter** to filter POS Devices by **Outlet**. Turn on **Activated Only** to display only activated POS Devices.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-105617.png>)

•	Select a POS Device and click **Edit** to update its details.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-105708.png>)

•	Select a POS Device and click **Delete** to remove it.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-105752.png>)

•	If a POS Device is activated, you can deactivate it by clicking **Edit** and changing its status.

**Note:** You can only deactivate an active POS Device.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-105844.png>)

\>Create an Outlet for your shop.

Creating an outlet is important because the outlet name and address will be displayed on your receipts. This setup needs to be done in the Management Hub.

Don’t know how to access Management Hub? *Click Here to learn how to access Management Hub.*

1\.	Go to Outlets > Outlets. Here, you can see all the outlets that have already been registered.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-110029.png>)

2\.	Click **New Outlet** to create a new outlet.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-110117.png>)

3\.	Enter the **Outlet Name** and **Phone Number**.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-110224.png>)

4\.	Enter the **Address** of the outlet.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-110321.png>)

5\.	Enter the **Postcode**.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-110404.png>)

6\.	Select the **Country**. By default, it is set to Malaysia.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-110507.png>)

7\.	Select the **State** and **City**.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-110621.png>)

8\.	In the **Description** box, you can leave any additional notes if needed.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-111007.png>)

9\.	Check that all the information entered is correct and Click **Create**.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-111050.png>)

10\.	Your **Outlet** has now been successfully created.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-111132.png>)

11\.	Optional: You can use the **Search button** to search for an outlet by name. You can also use the Filter button to filter outlets by City, State, or Country.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-111213.png>)
