Use these steps when opening the POS application for the first time or when signing in again.

1\.	Open the Mysztech POS application on the tablet or POS device.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-1.png>)

2\.	Enter the license key and select **Activate**.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-2.jpg>)

3\.	Enter your email address and password and click Sign In to your account to continue.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-3.jpg>)

4\.	Select your store from the dropdown list and choose Choose Store.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-4.jpg>)![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-5.jpg>)

5\.	Select Next.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-9.png>)

6\.	Select your POS device type from the dropdown list.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-7.jpg>)![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-8.jpg>)

7\.	Select Next.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-9.png>)

8\.	Enter your 6-digit PIN to authenticate Time Clock.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-10.jpg>)

9\.	Click OK to start your current shift.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-11.jpg>)

10\.	Enter your 6 digit PIN to CLOCK-IN to your current shift.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-12.jpg>)

11\.	Select Clock In button to proceed.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-13.jpg>)

12\.	Select Open Shift button to open your current shift.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-14.jpg>)

13\.	Check the details, then press Confirm to continue.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-15.jpg>)

14\.	Enter the starting cash amount in the cash drawer, then press OK.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-16.jpg>)

15\.	Select Open Shift button to continue.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-17.jpg>)

16\.	Wait for **“Syncing Data”** to reach 100%. Once it disappears, you can start making sales.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-18.jpg>)
