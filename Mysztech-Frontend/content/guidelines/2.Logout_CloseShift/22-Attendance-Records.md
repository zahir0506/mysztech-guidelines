The **Attendance** section keeps a record of staff attendance. Every time a staff member **clocks in** and **closes their shift**, the system records the **opening time, closing time**, and other relevant attendance details.

**View Attendance Records**

1\.	Go to **Employee > Attendance**. Here, you can view all recorded attendance records.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-115258.png>)

2\.	Use the **Search** button to search for a specific attendance record.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-115416.png>)

3\.	Click **Filter** to filter the records by **Outlet.**

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-115459.png>)

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-115537.png>)

4\.	Scroll down to view **the total number of results.**

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-115615.png>)

5\.	Use the **Per Page** option to choose how many records are displayed on each page. You can choose **10, 50, 100, or 200** records per page.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-115704.png>)

6\.	Use the page numbers at the bottom to move between pages, such as **Page 1, 2, 3, 4, 5,** and so on.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-115755.png>)
