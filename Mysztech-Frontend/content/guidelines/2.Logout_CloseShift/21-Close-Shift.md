There are two ways to close a shift: through the **POS System** or **Management Hub.**

**Recommended:** Close the shift directly from the **POS System**.

A. Close Shift from **POS System**

1\.	Open the Menu tab (on top- left – corner).

&#x20;![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Picture2-1.jpg>)

2\.	Select Shift button.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Picture2-2.jpg>)

3\.	Select Close Shift button to continue.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Picture2-3.jpg>)

4\.	Press Actual Cash Amount button (RM 0.00).

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Picture2-4.jpg>)

5\.	Calculate total amount in cash drawer correctly, then enter the actual cash amount and press OK.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Picture2-5.jpg>)

6\.	Enable Print Report and Print Sold Items button (if you want to print out current shift’s sales report and sold item), then select Close Shift.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Picture2-6.jpg>)

7\.	You have successfully completed the close shift process.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Picture2-7.jpg>)

B. Close Shift from **Management Hub**

**Note:** When selecting a shift, check the **Closing Time** column. If a shift appears without a closing time, it means that the shift has not been closed yet.

Don’t know how to access Management Hub, *click here to learn how to access Management Hub*

1\.	This section displays the shifts list. To close shift in the management hub, click the filter button located at the top right.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-114346.png>)

2\.	In the filter section, you can select any desired criteria and then click the apply button.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-114423.png>)

3\.	To view the details of a specific shift, you must click on the eye icons.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-114530.png>)

4\.	To close the selected shift,click on the close shift button.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-114612.png>)

5\.	Insert the actual cash amount into the provided field and then click close shift button.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-114656.png>)

6\.	Once completed, the system will display a notification indicating that the shift has been closed successfully.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-114738.png>)

7\.	To export the shift report, click on the export button located at the top right corner, select desired criteria, and then click the export button.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-114813.png>)

8\.	Once the export is completed, the system will display a confirmation notification and you can download the report.

![](</admin/2. Logout & Close Shift/Screenshot-2026-09-23-114843.png>)
