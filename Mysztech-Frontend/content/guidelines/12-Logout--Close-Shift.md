1\.	Enter your email address and password and click Sign In to your account to continue.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-3.jpg>)

2\.	Select your store from the dropdown list and choose Choose Store.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-4.jpg>)

3\.	Select Next.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-5.jpg>)

3\.	Select your POS device type from the dropdown list.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-7.jpg>)

4\.	Select Next.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-8.jpg>)

5\.	Enter your 6-digit PIN to authenticate Time Clock.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-8.jpg>)

6\.	Click OK to start your current shift.

![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-9.png>)

7\.	Enter your 6 digit PIN to CLOCK-IN to your current shift.

&#x20;![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-10.jpg>)

8\.	Select Clock In button to proceed.

&#x20;![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-11.jpg>)

9\.	Select Open Shift button to open your current shift.

&#x20;![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-12.jpg>)

10\.	Check the details, then press Confirm to continue.

&#x20;![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-13.jpg>)

11\.	Enter the starting cash amount in the cash drawer, then press OK.

&#x20;![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-14.jpg>)

12\.	Select Open Shift button to continue.

&#x20;![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-15.jpg>)

13\.	Wait for “Syncing Data” to reach 100%. Once it disappears, you can start making sales.

&#x20;![](</admin/1. Login & Open Shift/Picture1-16.jpg>)
