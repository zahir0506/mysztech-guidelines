The **Move Order** function allows you to move an order from one receipt to another receipt.

This is useful when customers want to combine their orders and make **one payment** instead of paying separately.

**Example**

When two separate orders need to be paid together, you can move order from a receipt  to the other receipt.

For example, **Customer A** wants to pay for **Customer B’s** order as well. Instead of making two separate payments, move the other order of **Customer B** to the **Customer A's** receipt. Both orders will then be combined into one bill.

**Steps to Move an Order**

1\.	Go to **Open Order**.

![](</admin/5. Manage Order /Screenshot-2026-09-17-172517.png>)

2\.	Select the square checkbox to the left of the **Order Number** you want to move. Do not click the order number itself, as that will only open the order.

**Example:** To move **Order No. 102** to **Order No. 101**, select the checkbox to the left of **Order No. 102** first.

![](</admin/5. Manage Order /Pic35.png>)

3\.	Tap **Move** at the top (right-arrow button) to move the selected order.

![](</admin/5. Manage Order /Screenshot-2026-09-17-172723.png>)

4\.	Select the **Order Number** you want to move the selected order to (e.g., **Order No. 101**).

![](</admin/5. Manage Order /Pic36.png>)

5\.	Tap **Confirm** to move **Order No. 102** to **Order No. 10**1 and proceed.

![](</admin/5. Manage Order /Pic37.png>)

6\.	You have successfully moved the order to another order. To verify, go to **Open Order** and tap the order number you moved it to (e.g., **Order No. 101**).

![](</admin/5. Manage Order /Pic38.png>)
