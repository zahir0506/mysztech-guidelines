You can manage your outlet’s suppliers from the **Suppliers** page. This setup needs to be done in the **Management Hub**.

Don’t know how to access Management Hub ? *Click Here to learn how to access Management Hub.*

1\.	Go to **Inventory > Suppliers**. This page will display all the registered suppliers.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-25-114354.png>)

2\.	Click **New Supplier** to create a new supplier.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-25-114502.png>)

3\.	Enter the supplier’s **Name** and **Business Registration Number.**

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-25-114550.png>)

4\.	Enter their **Email** and **Phone Number.**

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-25-114638.png>)

5\.	Select the **Settlement Type:**

o	**Cash**

o	**Credit**

o	**N/A** if other types apply.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-25-114737.png>)

6\.	Optionally, enter the supplier’s **Address** and add any notes in the **Description** box.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-25-114825.png>)

7\.	Select the **Outlet** that the supplier supplies.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-25-114915.png>)

8\.	Check the details and click **Create.**

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-25-115016.png>)

9\.	Your supplier has been successfully created.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-25-115058.png>)

10\.	You can also use the **Search** button to search for a supplier by name.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-25-115153.png>)

11\.	The **Column Adjustment** button allows you to choose which columns to display, such as **Name, Email, and Phone Number**. Click **Apply Columns** to save your selection.

![](</admin/8. Inventory Management & Logs/Screenshot-2026-09-25-115250.png>)
