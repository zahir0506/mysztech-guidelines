1\.	Press the **Edit** button at the top right.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Screenshot-2026-09-23-121811.png>)

2\.	Select the table you want to delete.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Screenshot-2026-09-23-121856.png>)

3\.	Press the **Delete** button to permanently delete the table.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Screenshot-2026-09-23-121940.png>)

4\.	Press **Save** to save your changes.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Screenshot-2026-09-23-122039.png>)

5\.	You have successfully deleted a table.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Screenshot-2026-09-23-122143.png>)
