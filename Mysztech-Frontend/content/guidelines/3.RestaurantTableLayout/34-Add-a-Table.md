1\.	 Select the section where you want to add table, then press the Edit button at the top right.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Picture3-24.jpg>)

2\.	Pick the table shape you like, then drag it into the section where you want the table.

&#x20;![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Picture3-25.jpg>)

3\.	Click on the table u just dragged, to edit the table.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Picture3-26.jpg>)

4\.	Type the table name, turn on Available if the table is ready, add the number of seats, and write notes in Remarks.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Picture3-27.jpg>)

5\.	Check the info you typed to make sure it’s correct, then press Save.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Picture3-28.jpg>)

6\.	You made a table. Now add more tables in any shape you like, until your section layout is complete.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Picture3-29.jpg>)

7\.	Tap the brush button below to add graphics or designs to your layout.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Picture3-30.jpg>)

8\.	Drag any decorative item from the bar below and drop it into your section to decorate the layout.

&#x20;![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Picture3-31.jpg>)

9\.	Drag a horizontal line (or any item) into your layout to divide tables into sections. Use these items as dividers or decorations to design your section the way you like.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Picture3-32.jpg>)

10\.	After you finish your design, press the Save button at the top right to keep the layout you made.

![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Picture3-33.jpg>)

11\.	You have successfully created your table layout.

&#x20;![](</admin/3. Restaurant Table Layout/Picture3-34.jpg>)
